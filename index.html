Driven by our scientific curiosity, we seek a deeper mechanistic knowledge of chemical systems and their applications in the modern world. Through associations within our group and with our collaborators, we are ambitiously driven to solve complex problems with worldwide implications.

The Kissel Research Group at Lewis University focuses on Surfaces and Interfaces, Electrochemistry, Inorganic Materials and Organic-Inorganic Compounds.
